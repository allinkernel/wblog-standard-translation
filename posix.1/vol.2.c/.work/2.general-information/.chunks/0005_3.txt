[h5_00686] [A423][/A423]SIG_IGN
[p_00687] Ignore signal.
[p_00688] Delivery of the signal shall have no effect on the process. The behavior of a process is undefined after it ignores
a SIGFPE, SIGILL, SIGSEGV, or SIGBUS signal that was not generated by [A424][I425]kill[/I425]()[/A424], [A426][I427]sigqueue[/I427]()[/A426], or [A428][I429]raise[/I429]()[/A428].
[p_00689] The system shall not allow the action for the signals SIGKILL or SIGSTOP to be set to SIG_IGN.
[p_00690] Setting a signal action to SIG_IGN for a signal that is pending shall cause the pending signal to be discarded,
whether or not it is blocked.
[p_00691] If a process sets the action for the SIGCHLD signal to SIG_IGN, the behavior is unspecified,

[p_00692] Any queued values pending shall be discarded and the resources used to queue them shall be released and made
